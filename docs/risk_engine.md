# Phase 10: Unified Risk Engine & Alert Prioritization

The Unified Risk Engine is responsible for consuming `Incident` objects (generated by the Cross-Threat Correlation engine) and producing transparent, mathematically derived risk scores (0-100), severity levels, and priority mappings. 

It explicitly keeps **confidence** (detector certainty) and **severity** (detector impact) separate from overall **Risk** (incident danger).

## Risk Formula
The system relies on a purely deterministic formula configurable in `config/risk.yaml`. No black-box machine learning is utilized.

```python
Risk Score = Base Severity Risk (Max alert severity)
           + Corroboration Bonus (Based on unique threat vectors)
           + Confidence Adjustment (Scaled around baseline)
           + Temporal Bonus (Derived from correlation_strength)
           + Progression Bonus (Derived from multi-stage mapping)
           + Metadata Quality Penalty (e.g., missing baselines)

Risk Score = CLAMP(Risk Score, 0.0, 100.0)
Risk Score = DECAY(Risk Score, days_inactive)
```

## Configurable Risk Levels
- **0–19**: `INFORMATIONAL` (P4)
- **20–39**: `LOW` (P4)
- **40–59**: `MEDIUM` (P3)
- **60–79**: `HIGH` (P2)
- **80–100**: `CRITICAL` (P1)

## Explainability
Instead of arbitrary classifications, every incident retains an array of `RiskFactor` objects detailing the exact numeric point contributions. 

A transparent textual summary is dynamically generated without asserting unverified claims (e.g. "Host is compromised" is avoided; instead, "Sequence of these indicators is consistent with potentially malicious multi-stage activity").

## Incident State & Lifecycle
Incidents default to `NEW`. If the risk engine scores an incident at 40 (MEDIUM) or above, its status is automatically promoted to `INVESTIGATING`. Risk history is appended to `risk_history` whenever the score meaningfully changes.

## Passive-Only Compliance
- `[PASS]` The engine relies entirely on in-memory computation of existing dataclasses.
- `[PASS]` No network egress or ingress is generated during risk scoring.
- `[PASS]` No automatic mitigations or actions are executed against the network.
